What I fix

The errors that stop an import or skew an analysis

The same ones that show up in CRM exports, merged lists from different people and downloads from government portals. Every issue is detected and counted before anything is changed.

Duplicates and blank rows

3 repeated rows→1 row

When no column identifies each row, I flag the duplicates for your review instead of silently deleting what may be real records.

Spaces and headers

"··Jane Smith·"→"Jane Smith"

Invisible spaces that break lookups, joins and VLOOKUPs are removed. Blank or repeated column names get fixed.

Placeholder values

N/A · Unknown · –→(empty)

Fillers become real empty cells, while genuine values survive: "NA" in a country-code column is Namibia.

Dates in mixed formats

22/09/2025→2025-09-22

Ambiguous dates such as 03/04/2025 are never guessed. I ask you whether they are day/month or month/day.

Numbers stored as text

"1,881,762"→1881762

Delivered Excel files contain real numbers you can sum. ZIP codes and IDs with leading zeros, such as 02134, stay intact.

Titles and footnotes

Source: Census→(removed)

Report-style files get their title rows and trailing notes removed, and two-level headers merged into one.
